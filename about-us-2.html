HISTORY



ITB Nigeria Limited was established in 1995 to address the growing demand for innovation and improved engineering expertise in Nigeria. With over two decades of experience in the construction industry, our teams of builders and civil engineering contractors have established ITB as the leading provider of top-quality construction services across the country. Our successful track record of delivered projects and strong relationships with our clients has reinforced our position as leaders in the market.

Quality



“This value has sustained our growth and reputation in the market.”





Central to all ITB's activities is the quality of works executed per time. We consistently aspire to provide quality solutions that meets and exceed the requirements and expectations of our clients. ISO 9001 :2015 forms the basis for the continuous improvement processes of the company's internal quality management system for which we are fully certified. Our high quality requirements are met by consistent application of our quality management in all areas and divisions of the company and is supported by all employees and top management. This has accounted for the obvious successes recorded on all our projects. Our team will continuously seek and develop new and improved ways to deliver products and services that are more efficient and effective, thus providing optimum value to our clients.



Safety



“Our HSE policies are reflected in how we conduct our activities.”





ITB Nigeria limited is committed to delivering a high level of Health, Safety and Environmental (HSE) performance and standards to ensure the protection of people, the environment and other business assets and interests.

To this end, ITB strives to achieve “Zero Accidents” by providing sufficient information, instruction, training and supervision to the workers, by instilling a strong safety culture through the exemplary leadership of site

management by clearly defining roles and responsibilities and also by minimizing our impact on the environment through pollution prevention, reduction of natural resource consumption and emissions and reduction of waste.

ITB’s HSE Management System is continually being strengthened by improving our procedures and practices and also by complying with applicable laws and regulations.
