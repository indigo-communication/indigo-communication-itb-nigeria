Eko Tower 2 (Guest House)





The Eko – Guest House owned by Eko Hotels, a design and build contract is set to be the tallest building in Victoria Island land at 118.34m from the finished ground Level. Situated along Ajose Adeogun Street and sitting next to the EKO Towers, the Eko Guest House edifice comprise the following: Basement, Ground floor, Mezzanine, Level 01-05 (car park), Level 06-07 (Emergency need centre), Level 08 (Restaurant), Level 09 (Restaurant/meeting room), Level 10 (Conference room), Level 11-12 (Health Club & Terrace), Level 13 and 26 (Technical Zone), Level 14 (staff Rooms) and Level 15-25 (Guest rooms).


No doubt the Eko Guest House is set to add colour to the beautiful Victoria Island landscape.

The structure consists of reinforced concrete columns, beams and Hollow Core slabs all sitting on piles and raft concrete foundation. All the finishing materials are first choice and carefully selected to meet the taste of the client.
