ABOUT US


ITB Nigeria Limited was established in 1995 to address the growing demand for innovation and improved engineering expertise in Nigeria.

With over two decades of experience in the construction industry, our teams of builders and civil engineering contractors have established ITB as the leading provider of top-quality construction services across the country. Our successful track record of delivered projects and strong relationships with our clients has reinforced our position as leaders in the market.
